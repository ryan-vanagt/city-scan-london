# London Policy Context

Reference document for grounding the City Doctor's responses in verified London
policy. All policies verified via GOV.UK, london.gov.uk, and tfl.gov.uk as of
April 2026. This file is loaded into the Doctor Agent's context to prevent policy
hallucination and provide evidence-based framing.

An organisation deploying CityScan would maintain this document with their own
active policies and priorities.

---

## Air Quality (Lungs)

- **London Environment Strategy** (GLA, 2018): Overarching strategy targeting WHO air quality
guidelines by 2030 and zero-emission city by 2050. Framework for ULEZ, Low Emission Bus
Zones, and Borough Air Quality Action Plans.
- **ULEZ London-wide** (TfL, expanded August 2023): £12.50/day charge for non-compliant
vehicles. Already covers ALL London boroughs. NOx emissions from cars/vans in outer London
13-16% lower since expansion. Next levers: enforcement intensity, expansion to heavier
vehicle classes.
- **Borough Air Quality Action Plans** (mandated under Environment Act 1995 / LLAQM framework):
All 33 London boroughs must declare Air Quality Management Areas and produce action plans.
Interventions include anti-idling zones, school streets, green corridors.
- **Mayor's Air Quality Fund**: £27M funding for borough-level air quality projects.

## Transport (Arteries)

- **Mayor's Transport Strategy** (GLA/TfL, 2018): 80% of trips by walking/cycling/public
transport by 2041. Vision Zero: eliminate all deaths and serious injuries on London's
transport network.
- **TfL Bus Action Plan** (TfL, 2022): Zero-emission bus fleet by 2034. 25km of new/improved
bus lanes by 2025. Superloop express orbital routes for outer London.
- **Step-Free Access Programme** (TfL, ongoing): 94 Tube stations now step-free (one-third of
network). All Elizabeth line and DLR stations step-free. Target: 50% reduction in additional
journey time for accessible routes by 2041.
- **Cycleways Programme** (TfL, ongoing): 400km+ network. £87.6M allocated 2025/26, £94.8M
for 2026/27. Cycling up 12.7% in 2025 vs 2024.

## Green Space (Skin)

- **London Plan Policy G4** (GLA, 2021): Protects existing open space from development.
Boroughs must conduct needs assessments and designate open space.
- **London Plan Policy G5 — Urban Greening Factor** (GLA, 2021): New major developments must
achieve UGF of 0.4 (residential) or 0.3 (commercial). Includes green roofs, walls, trees,
and nature-based drainage.
- **London Plan Policy G8 — Food Growing** (GLA, 2021): Protect allotments, encourage
community gardens and food growing in new developments.
- **All London Green Grid SPG** (GLA, 2012): Strategic framework for connected green spaces
across 11 Green Grid Area Frameworks. Still a material planning consideration.

## Crime & Safety (Immune)

- **Mayor's Police and Crime Plan 2025-2029** (MOPAC, 2025): "A Safer London for Everyone."
Neighbourhood policing with Town Centre Teams. Public health approach to violence. Reducing
violence against women and girls.
- **Violence Reduction Unit** (GLA, est. 2019): Public health approach to violence. £9.4M
Home Office funding secured February 2025. MyEnds community programme in highest-violence
wards. Youth workers embedded in trauma units (Teachable Moments).
- **Met Police Safer Neighbourhood Teams** (Met Police, ongoing): Ward-level dedicated teams
(sergeant + PCs + PCSOs). Expanded 2025/26 with 400 additional officers. Community surgeries
in libraries, family hubs, food banks.
- **Safer Streets Fund** (Home Office, 2020-2025): Five rounds, £159M total. Environmental
design: CCTV, street lighting, secured alleyways. Round 5 ended March 2025 — no Round 6
announced.

## Health & A&E (Immune)

- **NHS Long Term Plan** (NHS England, 2019): Urgent Treatment Centres as A&E alternative.
Same Day Emergency Care 12hrs/day 7 days/week. NHS 111 First to divert from A&E.
Note: partially superseded by "Fit for the Future: 10 Year Health Plan" (July 2025).
- **London Health Inequalities Strategy** (GLA, 2018, with 2025-2028 Implementation Plan):
Five themes: healthy children, healthy minds, healthy places, healthy communities, healthy
living. Address 20-year healthy life expectancy gap. Universal free school meals for primary
students. Health in All Policies approach.

## Deprivation / Cross-cutting

- **Fair Society, Healthy Lives — The Marmot Review** (Institute of Health Equity, 2010):
Six policy objectives for reducing health inequalities. Key principle: "proportionate
universalism" — universal services but scaled to deprivation level. Still the foundational
framework for health equity policy in England.
- **Health Equity in England: The Marmot Review 10 Years On** (Institute of Health Equity,
2020): Life expectancy stalled for first time in 100+ years. Health inequalities widened
2010-2020. Every £1 cut from local authority = estimated £10 in additional NHS costs.

## Public Realm (Brain)

- **Healthy Streets Approach** (TfL/Lucy Saunders, embedded in MTS 2018): 10 indicators
for street design: everyone feels welcome, easy to cross, shade and shelter, places to stop
and rest, not too noisy, people choose to walk/cycle, people feel safe, things to see and do,
people feel relaxed, clean air. All TfL schemes assessed against these.
- **Talk London** (GLA, est. 2013): Online community engagement platform. ~55,000 members.
Regular pulse surveys on quality of life, safety, transport, environment. Results feed
directly into GLA policy.
- **Good Growth by Design** (GLA, 2017): Design quality framework — inclusive, environmentally
responsive, connected, healthy. Mayor's Design Advocates (53 practitioners). London Review
Panel for design review of major developments.
